Project Overview

Full-Stack Software Development is a premier luxury fashion brand requiring a digital store that mirrors the elegance of haute couture. The goal was to build a fluid, ultra-fast online shopping experience with interactive lookbooks, mobile-first micro-interactions, and frictionless checkout.

Key Challenges

  • Heavy high-resolution image catalogs causing slow mobile page loads.
  • Complex product variant selectors for custom size and color combinations.
  • Integrating multi-currency payments and real-time inventory management.

Technical & Design Solutions

  • Implemented next-gen image compression with responsive srcset loading for sub-second rendering.
  • Designed a custom floating size-recommender drawer with smooth animations.
  • Engineered a streamlined 2-step checkout flow with multi-currency dynamic conversion.
